Voice Chat

Speak a request; Gemini responds aloud through your configured backend.

Gemini backend connection

The backend stores your Gemini key. Use Test backend to grant this extension access to that host.

Ready

Press Alt+Shift+V to start listening. Press it again while listening to cancel.

Conversation

Recognized text and saved voice-chat history are sent to the backend URL you configure, which forwards them to Google Gemini. The Gemini API key stays on that backend. Chrome speech recognition may process microphone audio through its recognition service. “Read aloud the conversation” reads this panel’s saved chat locally; this extension does not scrape ChatGPT or other webpage conversations.
